Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to making Arizon e commerce accessible to everyone.

Arizon e commerce is committed to providing a website that is accessible to the widest possible audience, regardless of ability or technology.

Standards

We aim to comply with WCAG 2.1 Level AA guidelines. Our site includes semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, descriptive alt text on images, sufficient color contrast, and an accessibility menu (look for the person icon on the right side of every page).

Accessibility Features

The accessibility menu lets you adjust text size, enable high contrast mode, reduce motion, and underline links — your preferences persist across pages.

In-Person Accessibility

Our restaurant is wheelchair accessible. Please contact us in advance for any specific accommodations and we will do our best to assist.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on our site or have suggestions, please let us know. We take all feedback seriously and aim to respond promptly.

Updates to This Document

Questions or Concerns
